## Decision accepted

ADR 0029 extends ADR 0018 and ADR 0027 to authorize Herdr as a supported client adapter. The decision approves the `.herdr-plugin/` manifest conventions, the `HERDR_PLUGIN_ROOT` root binding descriptor (`herdr-plugin-root/v1`), matrix integration in `v0.3.0.json`, and helper/launcher integration.
